# Permissions are Baseline ∩ Role ∩ Tenant grant ∩ Needs

Four layers, each owned by someone different, and narrowing only. The Baseline Policy is our hand-written floor and governs every run from the first click, including discovery. A Role is a named bundle per intent on a vendor app (`balance_reader`, `account_opener`), written ahead of time, which also binds to the least-privileged Service Account for that intent — so a read-only capability signs in as a login with no Transfer menu, and policy and infrastructure say the same thing. The Tenant grants roles and binds them to its own logins. Needs are derived from what a Discovery Run actually used and checked against the Role at approval, so a capability cannot quietly gain power in a later version. The cost is designing roles per vendor app up front; the benefit is that hundreds of tenants reuse them and every permission question has one owner.
